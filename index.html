I'm Emelia Ndhlovu, a BEng (Hons) Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from the University of Johannesburg, originally from Hwange, Zimbabwe, right at the edge of Victoria Falls. I grew up around game drives and school-holiday trips to the Falls before boarding school in Masvingo, where my Cambridge A-Level results opened the door to studying engineering in South Africa. Today I'm a registered ECSA Candidate Engineer with hands-on experience in power systems, automation, and IoT-based control, looking to grow within a structured graduate programme.
