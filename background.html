Background

Emelia Gamuchirai Ndhlovu was born in 1995 in Hwange, Zimbabwe, a town on the doorstep of Hwange National Park and a short trip from Victoria Falls. Childhood holidays were spent on game drives through the park and visits to the Falls — a upbringing that instilled an early appreciation for the natural world alongside a growing curiosity for how things work.

She attended boarding school at Riverton Academy in Masvingo, completing her Advanced Level studies under the Cambridge curriculum. Strong results there opened the door to further study in South Africa, where she went on to complete a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Johannesburg.

Today, Emelia is a graduate engineer and registered ECSA Candidate Engineer, exploring opportunities in global markets and looking to grow within a structured graduate engineering programme.
